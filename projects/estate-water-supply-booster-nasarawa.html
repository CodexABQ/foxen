Estate water supply, storage and booster system

Storage tanks, booster pumps and distribution pipework for a residential estate.

The challenge

A growing estate needed steady water pressure across many homes, with room to expand as new phases are built.

Our solution

We designed a storage and booster arrangement with spare capacity and isolation valves for each phase.

Scope of work

  • Storage tanks
  • Booster pump sets
  • Distribution pipework
  • Isolation and metering points
  • Pressure testing

The result

Phase one is in service and later phases are being connected as construction progresses.
