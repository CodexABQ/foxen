Frequently asked questions

What engineering services does FOXEN provide?

We provide mechanical and HVAC, electrical, plumbing and water, fire protection and industrial engineering, plus design and consultancy across all of them.

Do you provide complete MEP installation?

Yes. We can deliver mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection together as one coordinated package, or take on a single discipline.

Do you handle engineering design?

Yes. We prepare designs, calculations, drawings, specifications and bills of quantities, or work from your existing documents.

Where do you work?

We are based in Wuse Zone 5, Abuja, and travel to other states for projects.

Can FOXEN handle industrial projects?

Yes. We install process and utility piping, pumps, motors and plant services, and provide maintenance support.

How do I request a quotation?

Fill in the quote form on this page. It opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and you can attach drawings or photos in the chat. You can also call us.

What are your working hours?

We are open Monday to Friday. Messages sent on WhatsApp outside those days are answered on the next working day.
